Privacy Policy

Pazora · Last updated: November 5, 2026 · Developer: Jean Alves · Contact: jeanpoalves@outlook.com

Step data stays on your phone

The app counts steps with your phone's built-in step sensor. Your steps, water intake, goals, weight, height and history are stored only on this device. We do not have servers, we do not receive this data and we do not share it with anyone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Permissions

  • Physical activity: required to read the step sensor.
  • Notifications (optional): to show today's steps in the notification bar.
  • Run at startup / foreground service: to keep counting after a restart and while the app is closed.
  • Alarms: to send water reminders at the interval you choose (you can turn them off in Settings).

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as the advertising ID, approximate location from the IP address and ad interactions, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Brazil), the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice anytime in Settings > Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone settings.

Health information

Calories, distance and active time are estimates for general fitness purposes and are not medical advice. The app is not a medical device.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

Because step data never leaves your device, you control it fully. For questions about this policy or about data processed by advertising partners, contact us at the email above.

Política de Privacidade

Pazora · Última atualização: 5 de novembro de 2026 · Desenvolvedor: Jean Alves

Os dados de passos ficam no seu celular. O app conta passos com o sensor do aparelho; passos, água, metas, peso, altura e histórico ficam só neste dispositivo. Os lembretes de água podem ser desligados em Ajustes. Não temos servidores e não recebemos nem compartilhamos esses dados. Desinstalar o app apaga tudo.

Anúncios: o app exibe anúncios do Google AdMob, que pode coletar o identificador de publicidade, localização aproximada pelo IP e interações com anúncios, conforme a Política de Privacidade do Google. Quando a lei exige (ex.: LGPD, GDPR), o app pede seu consentimento, e você pode mudar a escolha em Ajustes > Privacidade de anúncios.

Calorias, distância e tempo ativo são estimativas e não são orientação médica. O app não é direcionado a menores de 13 anos. Contato: jeanpoalves@outlook.com.
